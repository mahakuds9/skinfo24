My Story

I am Saroj Kumar Mahakud, an Application Support Engineer with 5+ years of experience in IT support, incident management, and IT operations. I started my journey with a background in Chemistry and transitioned into the IT industry — proving that passion and dedication can take you anywhere.

Over the years I have worked with global clients, managed critical incidents, handled escalations, and built strong expertise in Linux, Python, Shell Scripting, MySQL and cloud technologies like AWS.

I believe in continuous learning and I am always looking to grow — which is why I am currently building real-world projects to strengthen my practical skills.
